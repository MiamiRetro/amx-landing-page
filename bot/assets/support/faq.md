# BLKBöX Trading Floor: help-desk FAQ

The support assistant answers only from this file. Keep each answer short and
factual. Write in English; the assistant replies in the member's language.

Each entry is a "## question" heading followed by its answer. Optional lines:
- "Also asked:" other ways members ask the same thing, to help matching.
- "Guide:" the infographic the bot attaches with the answer (see the list in
  src/support/guides.ts). Use a guide only where the picture helps.
- "Mods:" notes for moderators. The assistant never quotes these.

# Getting around Discord

## I'm new to Discord. How do I get around?
Also asked: how does Discord work, where is everything, I'm lost, how do I use this app
Channels are the rooms listed down the side. On desktop they are always on the
left. On phone, tap the ☰ menu at the top left (or swipe right) to see them.
Your language's channels are grouped in sections like Signals & Alerts and
Trading Floor; tap a section name to open or close it. The 🧭 SERVER-GUIDE channel at the
top explains everything step by step.
Guide: discord-mobile

## How do I use Discord on my phone?
Also asked: mobile app, iPhone, Android, where are the channels on my phone, how do I go back
Swipe right, or tap ☰ at the top left, to open the server and channel list.
Tap a channel to open it, and swipe right again to go back to the list. The
server icons are in the narrow column on the far left. Threads show under
their channel, and the threads icon at the top right of a channel lists them.
Guide: discord-mobile

## How do I use Discord on a computer?
Also asked: desktop app, browser, PC, Mac, web version
Server icons run down the far left. Next to them is the channel list for the
server you are in. The middle is the chat, and the member list is on the
right. Threads show under their channel in the list, and the threads icon at
the top of a channel lists all of them. Discord works in a browser at
discord.com/app too.
Guide: discord-desktop

## What is a channel, a category and a thread?
Also asked: what does # mean, what's the difference between channels and threads
A channel is one chat room, shown with a # or an icon. A category is a
folder of channels, like Signals & Alerts or Trading Floor. A thread is a smaller
side conversation inside a channel; your help thread is one of these.

## Which channel should I go to first?
Also asked: where do I start, what should I read first
Start with the 🧭 SERVER-GUIDE channel at the very top of your channel list. It walks
you through the rules, how to sign up, and how to verify.
Guide: channel-map

## Where is the server guide?
Also asked: server guide missing, start here
It is the channel with the 🧭 compass and bold capitals at the very top of your
channel list, above every section. On phone, open the channel list with ☰ and scroll to the top.
Guide: channel-map

## What is in each channel?
Also asked: channel list explained, what are all these channels for
Announcements carries news from the team. General is open chat and memes is
for fun. help-desk is where you open a help thread. The 🔒 channels hold the
signals and the trading group, and open once you verify your UID. The 🎓
academy, at the very bottom of the Trading Floor, holds setup guides, trading
strategies and video tutorials: everyone can read it, and verified members can
post and reply.
Guide: channel-map

## How do I find my help thread again?
Also asked: I lost my thread, where did my ticket go, my thread disappeared
Tap the link the bot sent you, or open help-desk: your thread is listed under
the channel name (on phone, tap help-desk and then the threads icon at the top
right). Threads you are in also show in the channel list with a small
indent. If you closed it, press Get help again for a new one.
Guide: help-thread

## How do I reply or send a screenshot?
Also asked: how do I attach a picture, upload an image, send a photo, share my screen
Type in the message box at the bottom of the thread and press Enter (send on
phone). To add a screenshot, tap the + next to the message box and pick the
image. Never post a screenshot that shows a password, API key or seed phrase.
Guide: help-thread

## How do I take a screenshot?
Also asked: how do I screenshot on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac
iPhone: press the side button and volume up together. Android: power and
volume down together. Windows: Windows key + Shift + S. Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4.
Then attach it with the + next to the message box.

## Why can't I type in help-desk?
Also asked: I can't send messages in help-desk, help-desk is read-only
help-desk itself is only for opening threads, so nobody chats in the channel.
Press Get help on the pinned message and type in the private thread it opens.
Guide: help-thread

## Why can't I type in a channel?
Also asked: I can't send messages, the message box is grey, no permission to send
Announcements, signals and the 🔒 channels are read-only. You can chat in
general and memes once you have picked a language, and in the trading group
channels once you verify your UID.

## Why can't I see some channels?
Also asked: channels are missing, where are the signals, I can only see a few channels
Channels with a 🔒 are for verified members, and your language's channels are
the only ones shown to you. Verify your UID to open the rest.
Guide: channel-map

## I'm not getting notifications.
Also asked: no alerts, how do I get pinged, turn on notifications
Long-press (phone) or right-click (desktop) the server icon, open Notification
Settings and choose All Messages or Only @mentions. Your phone's own settings
must also allow Discord notifications.

## There are too many notifications.
Also asked: how do I mute, stop pings, too many alerts
Long-press (phone) or right-click (desktop) a channel or the server icon and
choose Mute, or set notifications to Only @mentions.

## How do I change my nickname or profile picture?
Also asked: change name, avatar, display name
Tap your profile picture (bottom right on phone, bottom left on desktop) to
edit your profile. For this server only, long-press or right-click the server
icon and choose Edit Per-server Profile.

## The server looks different on my friend's phone.
Also asked: my friend sees other channels, why is my server different
Each member sees the channels for their own language, and verified members
see more than unverified ones. That is normal.

## My verification failed. What now?
Also asked: verification not working, it says it can't see my UID, verify failed, error

## My account wasn't opened through your link.
Also asked: I already had an account, I signed up before, I used another referral, can you add my old account

server guide, then verify that new UID.

## It says that doesn't look like a UID.

# Signals and channels

## What are the AlphanumetriX signals?
Also asked: what is AMX, what is AlphanumetriX, what does the indicator do
AlphanumetriX is the BLKBöX flagship indicator. It looks for trends and
exhaustion points and gives bullish and bearish signals on any market and
timeframe. Verified members see the full signals in the signals channels and
the app.

## What do the colours on the alphanumetrix channel mean?
Also asked: green green, red yellow, channel icon, 🟢🟢 🟡🟢 🔴🟡 🔴🔴
The icon shows how many of the indicator's three main signals are bullish
right now. 🟢🟢 strong bull trend, 🟡🟢 leaning bullish, 🔴🟡 leaning bearish,
🔴🔴 strong bear trend.

## What is the difference between the signal channels?
Also asked: trade alerts vs bot alerts, which channel has signals
Trade alerts carry the trade alerts. Bot alerts come from the BLKBöX trading
bots. AlphanumetriX shows the flagship indicator's state. All three open once
you verify.

## What timeframe do the signals use?
Also asked: which timeframe, daily or 4 hour
AlphanumetriX works on any timeframe. Position traders tend to use the daily
or 3-day chart, and swing traders the 4-hour.

## Do I need TradingView?
Also asked: TradingView account, where do I see the indicator
The AlphanumetriX indicator runs on TradingView, so using it on your own
charts needs a TradingView account (a free one works to start). The signals
in Discord and the app need nothing extra.

## What is the trading group?
Also asked: bitcoin channel, crypto channel, stocks, commodities
The trading group is the bitcoin, crypto, stocks and commodities chats. They
open once you verify your UID.

## What is in the academy?
Also asked: reference, forums, tutorials, setup guides, trading strategies, videos
The 🎓 academy is one forum at the bottom of the Trading Floor. Each post has a tag:
Setup guide (the tools step by step), Strategy (how the team uses the
signals), Video tutorial, Question and Discussion. Tap a tag at the top of the
forum to see only those posts. Everyone can read it; verified members can post
and reply.

## Can I ask questions about a trade?
Also asked: what do you think about this coin, chart review
You can discuss trades in the trading group channels once verified. Nobody
here gives financial advice; decisions are yours.

# Safety and scams

## Someone asked me for my API key or password.
Also asked: someone DMed me, support asked for my login, is this admin real
Never share an API key, password or seed phrase with anyone, including people
who say they are staff. Staff will never ask. Report it to a moderator here.
Guide: scams

## Someone DMed me offering to trade for me or double my money.
Also asked: investment offer in DM, account manager, recovery service, guaranteed returns
That is a scam. Staff never DM first to offer trading, investments or fund
recovery. Don't reply, block them, and report it to a moderator here.
Guide: scams

## How do I know someone is really staff?
Also asked: is this a real moderator, fake admin
Real staff have a moderator role colour in the member list and only help in
this server, never in DMs asking for money or logins. If in doubt, ask here.
Guide: scams

## I think I got scammed.
Also asked: I lost money, I sent money to someone, hacked
Sorry this happened. Stop all contact with them, change your passwords and
turn on 2FA, and contact your exchange's own support. Ask for a moderator here
so we can warn others and remove the account.

## How do I stop DMs from strangers?
Also asked: block DMs, privacy settings
Long-press or right-click the server icon, open Privacy Settings and turn off
Direct Messages from server members.
Guide: scams

## How do I report someone?
Also asked: report a user, someone is harassing me, spam
Ask for a moderator here and say who it is and what happened; screenshots
help. You can also right-click or long-press their message and choose Report.
