# BLKBöX help-desk FAQ

The AI help-desk answers only from this file. Anything it does not cover goes to a moderator.
Write each answer in English; the assistant answers in the member's own language.
Edit freely: the bot reads this file on every question, so changes apply without a restart.

## What is BLKBöX?
BLKBöX is a crypto trader's command center: market intelligence, proprietary signals (AlphanumetriX), multi-exchange execution and a trade journal, in one app at https://app.blkbox.pro. It is free. No card and no subscription.

## Is it really free? How do you make money?
Yes. You pay trading fees to whatever exchange you trade on anyway. When you open your exchange account through one of our affiliate links, the exchange gives us a small share of those fees. It never costs you anything extra, and it is what pays for the signals, bots and tools.

## How do I get full access to the server?
Three steps:
1. Create your free account at https://www.blkbox.pro/signup.
2. Open an exchange account through one of our links (Bitget, Blofin or Bybit). The links are in the server guide channel.
3. Verify your exchange UID: press the Verify button in any 🔒 channel (for example 🔒 trading-group or 🔒 trade-signals), pick your exchange and paste your UID.
Once verified, every locked channel opens straight away.

## What is a UID and where do I find it?
Your UID is your exchange account number. It is not a password and not an API key. You find it in your exchange profile or account page, usually at the top next to your name or avatar. The verify panel has a picture showing where it is.

## Which exchange should I pick?
Pick based on where you live:
- Europe and the UK: Bybit or Blofin.
- Korea: Bybit, Blofin or MEXC.
- Indonesia and Japan: Bybit, Blofin, MEXC or Toobit.
- Anywhere else: any of them; Bybit is the easiest start.
Bybit and Bitget need an ID check. Blofin needs no ID check and futures are open at signup. MEXC and Toobit are coming soon.

## I'm in the US or Canada. Can I join?
The exchanges we work with exclude US and Canadian residents in their own terms, so we cannot verify accounts from there yet. The team is working on an option for you.

## My UID won't verify.
Common reasons:
- The account was not opened through our affiliate link. Only accounts opened through our links can be verified.
- The UID was typed wrong, or it is from a different exchange than the one you picked.
- The UID was already verified by another Discord account.
- Too many tries in a short time: wait fifteen minutes and try again.
If none of these fit, ask for a moderator and they will check it by hand.

## I already have an exchange account that wasn't opened through your link.
Exchanges only credit accounts opened through the referral link. The usual fix is to open a new account through our link and verify that UID. A moderator can help if you are unsure.

## Which exchanges are accepted for verification?
Bitget, Blofin and Bybit UIDs are accepted today. MEXC and Toobit are coming soon.

Channels marked 🔒 are for verified members. Press the Verify button inside one of them and verify your exchange UID to unlock everything.

## Can I change my language?
Your language choice is final from the picker, but a moderator can change it for you. Ask for a moderator.

## Is any of this financial advice?
No. Alerts, bots, calls and chat are education and opinion. You decide what you trade and the risk is yours alone. Nobody in the server is responsible for your funds.

## Someone DMed me offering help, signals or an investment.
The team will never DM you first, ask for your password, seed phrase or API keys, or ask you to send funds. Do not reply, and report the user to a moderator.

## How do I use the app (app.blkbox.pro)?
Sign in at https://app.blkbox.pro. Drop your exchange UID into the app to turn on the AlphanumetriX signals, the trade terminal and the trade journal. The 📕 setup-guides channel has step-by-step guides.
