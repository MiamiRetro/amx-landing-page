# BLKBöX Trading Floor: help-desk FAQ

The support assistant answers only from this file. Keep each answer short and
factual. Write in English; the assistant replies in the member's language.
Lines starting with "Mods:" are notes for moderators and are never quoted.

## I'm new to Discord. How do I get around?
Channels are the rooms listed down the side. On desktop they are always on the
left. On phone, tap the ☰ menu at the top left (or swipe right) to see them.
Your language's channels are grouped in sections like Announcements and
Community; tap a section name to open or close it. The 【server guide】 at the
top explains everything step by step.

## How do I find my help thread again?
Tap the link the bot sent you, or open help-desk: your thread is listed under
the channel name (on phone, tap help-desk and then the threads icon at the top
right). Threads you are in also show in the channel list with a small
indent.

## How do I reply or send a screenshot?
Type in the message box at the bottom of the thread and press Enter (send on
phone). To add a screenshot, tap the + next to the message box and pick the
image. Never post a screenshot that shows a password, API key or seed phrase.

## Why can't I see some channels?
Channels with a 🔒 are for verified members, and your language's channels are
the only ones shown to you. Verify your UID to open the rest.

## I'm not getting notifications.
Long-press (phone) or right-click (desktop) the server icon, open Notification
Settings and choose All Messages or Only @mentions. Your phone's own settings
must also allow Discord notifications.

## What is the BLKBöX Trading Floor?
A free community for crypto traders, run by BLKBöX (blkbox.pro). Members get
the AlphanumetriX signals, bot alerts, the trading group chats and the BLKBöX command
center app. It is education and tools, not financial advice.

## Why is it free?
When you sign up to an exchange through one of our links, the exchange shares
a small part of the trading fees you already pay with us. That pays for the
signals, bots and tools. It never costs you anything extra.

## How do I unlock the full server?
Open an account on a partner exchange through one of our links, then verify
your UID. Press "Verify my UID" in any 🔒 channel, pick your exchange and paste
your UID. Verification is instant, and the locked channels open right away.

## Which exchanges can I verify with?
Bybit, Bitget and Blofin. MEXC and Toobit are coming soon. The server guide at
the top of your channel list has the sign-up links.

## Which exchange should I pick?
Pick by where you live. Europe and the UK: Bybit or Blofin. Anywhere else: any
of them; Bybit is the easiest start. Blofin needs no ID check. Bybit and Bitget
need an ID check.

## Can I join from the United States or Canada?
Every partner exchange excludes US and Canadian residents in its own terms, so
we can't verify accounts from there yet. We are working on an option.

## Where do I find my UID?
In the exchange app, open your profile or account page. The UID is the account
number shown near your name, usually with a copy button. It is a number, not a
password and not an API key.

## My verification failed. What now?
The most common reason is that the account was not opened through one of our
links, so the exchange does not show it to us. Check you picked the right
exchange and pasted the UID with no spaces. If it still fails, ask for a
moderator here.
Mods: use /uid check to see what the exchange returned for that UID.

## It says my UID is already used.
Each UID can be linked to one Discord account. If it is yours and you changed
Discord accounts, a moderator can move it across. Ask for a moderator here.

## How do I change my language?
Your language is locked once you pick it, so the server stays consistent for
you. A moderator can change it for you. Ask for a moderator here.
Mods: /language member.

## Why are messages in my language?
The bot translates chat between the language sections automatically, so
everyone can talk to everyone. Messages are sent to a translation service to do
this.

## What are the AlphanumetriX signals?
AlphanumetriX is the BLKBöX flagship indicator. Its channel icon shows how many
of its signals are bullish right now. Verified members see the full signals in
the signals channels and the app.

## What is the difference between the signal channels?
Trade signals carry the trade alerts. Bot alerts come from the BLKBöX trading
bots. AlphanumetriX shows the flagship indicator's state. All three open once
you verify.

## Is this financial advice?
No. Everything here is education and tools. Do your own research and only
trade what you can afford to lose.

## Someone asked me for my API key or password.
Never share an API key, password or seed phrase with anyone, including people
who say they are staff. Staff will never ask. Report it to a moderator here.

## What is the BLKBöX command center?
The BLKBöX app: market intelligence, signals, multi-exchange trading and a
trade journal in one place. Sign up from the server guide. It is free.

## How do I talk to a person?
Press "Talk to a mod" in this thread and a moderator will join.
